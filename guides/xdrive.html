xDrive Explained (AWD)

What is xDrive?

xDrive is BMW’s AWD system, distributing torque electronically for grip and stability.

Benefits

  • All-weather security
  • Improved launches
  • Balanced handling in corners

Evolution

Earlier systems were mechanical; now they are computer-controlled with predictive sensors, integrating with DSC stability systems.

Performance Use

M cars with xDrive can send most torque to rear wheels for playful handling.

FAQs

  • Is xDrive full-time? Yes, but it can bias rearward heavily when conditions allow.
