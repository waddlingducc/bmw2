Maintenance & Reliability Basics

Intervals

Modern BMWs use Condition Based Service (CBS). Oil change intervals ~10k miles, brake fluid every 2 years.

Tires

Many BMWs use staggered tire setups. Rotate only front-to-back if square; budget $1k+ for a full set.

Common Repairs

  • Water pumps on older models
  • Suspension bushings
  • High-voltage battery checks for EVs

Warranties

4yr/50k standard; 8yr/100k for EV batteries.

Tips

  • Use OEM filters/fluids
  • Document all service
  • Consider extended warranty for peace of mind

FAQs

  • Are BMWs reliable? Newer models improved; EVs have fewer moving parts.
