Ownership Costs & TCO

Overview

Total Cost of Ownership includes purchase, fuel/energy, insurance, depreciation, and maintenance.

Fuel vs Electricity

EVs cost ~40–60% less to “fuel” per mile. Gas M cars consume premium fuel at higher rates.

Insurance

Rates depend on driver/location; M cars trend higher, EVs sometimes too due to repair complexity.

Depreciation

BMWs retain value well, but EV residuals are still forming. Special editions hold strongest.
