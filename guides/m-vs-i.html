M vs i: Performance vs Electric

Introduction

BMW’s M and i lineups represent two philosophies: high-performance combustion vs cutting-edge electrification.

M Models

  • Engines: Turbocharged inline-sixes, V8s
  • Performance: 0–60 often under 4s
  • Feel: Loud exhaust, sharp handling

i Models

  • Motors: Dual electric motors common
  • Performance: Instant torque, smooth
  • Feel: Silent, futuristic, efficient

Ownership

M cars cost more in fuel/tires; i cars save on running costs but need charging access.
