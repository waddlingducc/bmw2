DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW M2 is the rowdy, compact M coupe that channels the spirit of classic BMW performance. Powered by the S58 twin-turbo inline-six and available with a 6-speed manual, it’s a driver’s car that balances everyday usability with track-ready punch.

Highlights

  • S58 3.0L twin-turbo inline-six producing 450+ hp
  • Choice of 6-speed manual or 8-speed automatic
  • Rear-wheel drive for classic BMW handling feel
  • Adaptive M suspension and M brakes standard
  • Compact footprint for agility and precise steering

Specs

  • Powertrain: Twin-turbo 3.0L I6 (gas)
  • Drivetrain: RWD
  • Transmissions: 6-speed manual or 8-speed automatic
  • 0–60 mph: — 4.1 sec

Pros

  • Engaging, playful chassis with RWD dynamics
  • Manual transmission option for purists
  • Compact size makes it fun on tight roads and tracks
  • Latest iDrive and driver assistance features

Consider

  • Rear seats are tight for adults
  • Firm suspension may be harsh on rough roads
  • Less trunk space than larger M cars
