DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW X5 xDrive40i is a refined midsize luxury SUV that balances power, technology, and comfort. Powered by a turbocharged inline-six with mild-hybrid assist and standard all-wheel drive, it delivers smooth acceleration, a premium interior, and the versatility to handle family duty or long-distance cruising.

Highlights

  • Turbocharged 3.0L inline-six with 48V mild-hybrid system
  • Standard xDrive AWD for confident all-weather traction
  • Optional third-row seating for added passenger capacity
  • Spacious, upscale cabin with premium finishes
  • BMW curved display with iDrive 8 infotainment

Specs

  • Powertrain: Turbo 3.0L I6 with mild-hybrid (gas)
  • Drivetrain: xDrive AWD
  • Transmission: 8-speed automatic
  • 0–60 mph: — 5.2 sec

Pros

  • Strong and smooth inline-six with hybrid assist
  • Luxurious and spacious interior with high-quality features
  • Optional third row increases family flexibility
  • Balanced mix of performance, comfort, and tech

Consider

  • Third row is tight compared to full-size SUVs
  • Price rises quickly with options and packages
  • Heavier than smaller BMW SUVs, reducing agility
