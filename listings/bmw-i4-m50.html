DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW i4 M50 is the performance-tuned version of BMW’s all-electric Gran Coupe. With dual motors delivering all-wheel drive, over 500 horsepower, and M-specific chassis tuning, it blends EV efficiency with the excitement of M Performance driving.

Highlights

  • Dual-motor all-wheel drive with ~536 hp
  • Sport-tuned suspension and M Performance brakes
  • Estimated 227–267 miles of range
  • BMW curved display with iDrive 8.5 infotainment
  • Fast charging up to 200 kW DC

Specs

  • Powertrain: Dual-motor electric AWD
  • Output: ~536 hp / ~586 lb-ft torque
  • Battery: ~81 kWh usable capacity
  • Range: ~227–267 miles (EPA est.)
  • 0–60 mph: — 3.7 sec

Pros

  • M Performance acceleration with instant EV torque
  • Gran Coupe body offers practicality with sporty looks
  • Quiet, refined cabin with premium features
  • Compatible with fast charging for road trips

Consider

  • Less range than some rivals in the segment
  • Heavier than gas-powered 4 Series due to battery weight
  • Price climbs quickly with M Performance options
