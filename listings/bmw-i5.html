DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW i5 eDrive40 is the all-electric version of the 5 Series sedan, combining luxury and long-range efficiency. With sleek styling, a tech-rich interior, and advanced driver assistance, it delivers a refined EV experience without sacrificing BMW’s hallmark driving dynamics.

Highlights

  • All-electric 5 Series sedan with single-motor rear-wheel drive
  • Estimated 270–295 miles of range
  • BMW curved display with iDrive 8.5 and advanced infotainment
  • Fast charging capability up to 205 kW DC
  • Luxury-focused interior with sustainable material options

Specs

  • Powertrain: Single electric motor (rear-wheel drive)
  • Output: ~335 hp
  • Battery: ~84.3 kWh usable capacity
  • Range: ~270–295 miles
  • 0–60 mph: — 5.7 sec

Pros

  • Refined, quiet electric driving experience
  • Spacious and luxurious 5 Series interior
  • Fast charging with BMW Charging network compatibility
  • Balances efficiency, comfort, and modern tech

Consider

  • Less performance than the dual-motor i5 M60 variant
  • Higher price compared with gas-powered 530i
  • Charging infrastructure varies by region
