DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW 330i continues the 3 Series legacy as the benchmark sport sedan. With a turbocharged four-cylinder, rear- or all-wheel drive, and balanced chassis, it blends sharp handling with comfort and modern tech, making it a versatile daily driver and enthusiast favorite.

Highlights

  • Turbocharged 2.0L four-cylinder with strong efficiency and power
  • Choice of rear-wheel drive or xDrive all-wheel drive
  • 8-speed automatic with smooth, responsive shifts
  • Modern interior with curved display and iDrive 8 infotainment
  • Driver assistance features like adaptive cruise and lane-keep

Specs

  • Powertrain: Turbo 2.0L inline-four (gas)
  • Drivetrain: RWD or AWD (xDrive)
  • Transmission: 8-speed automatic
  • 0–60 mph: — 5.6 sec

Pros

  • Balanced ride and handling — sporty yet comfortable
  • Strong turbo engine with good fuel economy
  • Refined interior with latest tech and infotainment
  • Versatile choice: commuter-friendly yet engaging

Consider

  • Less thrilling than higher trims (M340i or M3)
  • Options can quickly raise the price
  • Rear seat space smaller than larger sedans (5 Series)
