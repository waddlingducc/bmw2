DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW M3 Competition xDrive takes the iconic M3 sedan and adds all-wheel drive traction. With over 500 horsepower from the S58 twin-turbo inline-six, blistering acceleration, and configurable AWD modes, it’s a super-sedan that balances track performance with year-round usability.

Highlights

  • S58 3.0L twin-turbo inline-six producing 500+ hp
  • Standard xDrive all-wheel drive with rear-bias tuning
  • Adaptive M suspension and powerful M brakes
  • 8-speed automatic with launch control
  • Modern cabin with curved display, iDrive 8, and M-specific modes

Specs

  • Powertrain: Twin-turbo 3.0L I6 (gas)
  • Drivetrain: xDrive AWD with adjustable rear-bias
  • Transmission: 8-speed M Steptronic automatic
  • 0–60 mph: — 3.4 sec

Pros

  • Supercar-level performance in a four-door sedan
  • AWD traction for all-weather confidence
  • Configurable drive modes for track or comfort
  • Spacious and practical compared to coupes

Consider

  • Bold styling may not appeal to traditionalists
  • No manual transmission option in Competition trim
  • Higher starting price than RWD base M3
