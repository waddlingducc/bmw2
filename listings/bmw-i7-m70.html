DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW i7 M70 is the ultimate luxury EV sedan in BMW’s lineup, combining the flagship 7 Series design with M-tuned electric performance. With dual motors producing over 640 horsepower, an ultra-luxurious cabin, and advanced driver assistance, it delivers both limousine comfort and exhilarating speed.

Highlights

  • Dual-motor electric AWD with ~650 hp
  • Flagship luxury sedan with M Performance tuning
  • Estimated 270–300 miles of EPA range (verify on BMWUSA)
  • Executive lounge seating with rear-seat entertainment options
  • BMW curved display with iDrive 8.5 and M-specific graphics
  • Fast charging up to 195 kW DC

Specs (verify on BMWUSA)

  • Powertrain: Dual-motor electric AWD
  • Output: ~650 hp / ~748 lb-ft torque
  • Battery: ~101.7 kWh usable capacity
  • Range: 268-285 miles
  • 0–60 mph: — 3.5 sec

Pros

  • Ultra-luxurious interior with high-end materials and tech
  • M Performance dynamics in a full-size EV sedan
  • Rear-seat luxury options rival high-end limousines
  • Powerful dual-motor system with instant acceleration

Consider

  • Expensive compared to smaller BMW EVs
  • Large size may feel less agile than smaller sedans
  • Heavy weight impacts efficiency compared to lighter EVs
