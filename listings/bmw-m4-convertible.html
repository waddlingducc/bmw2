DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The M4 Convertible brings the S58 twin-turbo inline-six to an open-top platform, mixing serious pace with top-down cruising comfort. Expect adaptive M suspension, powerful brakes, and the latest infotainment.

Highlights

  • S58 3.0L twin-turbo I6 (M tune)
  • Adaptive M suspension and performance differential
  • iDrive with curved display and driver assistance options
  • Power soft top with quick operation

Specs

  • Powertrain: High-output turbo I6 (gas)
  • Drivetrain: RWD/AWD (varies by trim/model year)
  • 0–60 mph: — 3.6 sec

Pros

  • Serious M performance with open-air appeal
  • Strong, responsive powertrain
  • Modern tech and assistance features

Consider

  • Less cargo space vs. coupe
  • Summer tires may need a winter set
