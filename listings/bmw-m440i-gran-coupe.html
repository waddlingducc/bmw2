DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW M440i xDrive Gran Coupe blends coupe-like styling with four-door practicality and M Performance power. With the B58 turbocharged inline-six, xDrive AWD, and a sleek hatchback design, it delivers both sporty thrills and everyday versatility.

Highlights

  • B58 3.0L turbocharged inline-six with 386 hp
  • xDrive all-wheel drive standard for traction and confidence
  • Gran Coupe hatchback design adds utility and rear-seat space
  • Sport-tuned suspension and available adaptive dampers
  • BMW curved display with iDrive infotainment and driver assists

Specs (verify on BMWUSA)

  • Powertrain: Turbocharged 3.0L I6 (gas)
  • Drivetrain: xDrive AWD
  • Transmission: 8-speed automatic
  • 0–60 mph: — 4.3 sec

Pros

  • Strong, smooth power from the B58 inline-six
  • Practical hatchback design without losing sporty looks
  • All-weather traction from xDrive AWD
  • Premium interior with advanced tech and comfort

Consider

  • Less cargo height than a traditional SUV
  • No manual transmission option
  • Price climbs quickly with packages and options
