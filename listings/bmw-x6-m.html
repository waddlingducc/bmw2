Quick overview: The X6 M Competition pairs a high-output V8 with M xDrive, adaptive suspension, and aggressive coupe-SUV styling for serious pace and daily practicality.

Highlights

  • 4.4-liter M TwinPower Turbo V8 with 48V mild-hybrid assist
  • M xDrive AWD with performance rear bias and active M differential
  • Adaptive M Suspension Professional and powerful M brakes
  • Curved display with iDrive and driver assistance options

Specs

  • Powertrain: High-output twin-turbo V8 (gas)
  • Drivetrain: AWD

Pros

  • Super-SUV acceleration and handling
  • All-weather traction with M tuning
  • Upscale, tech-rich interior

Consider
