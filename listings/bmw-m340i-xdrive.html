BMW M340I XDRIVE SEDAN

DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW M340i xDrive Sedan sits between the standard 3 Series and the full M3. Powered by the beloved B58 inline-six and equipped with xDrive AWD, it offers thrilling performance, everyday comfort, and modern tech — making it a sweet spot in BMW’s lineup.

Highlights

  • B58 3.0L turbocharged inline-six with 386 hp
  • xDrive all-wheel drive for year-round traction
  • 8-speed automatic with launch control
  • M Sport differential and adaptive suspension available
  • BMW curved display with iDrive 8 infotainment

Specs

  • Powertrain: Turbocharged 3.0L I6 (gas)
  • Drivetrain: xDrive AWD
  • Transmission: 8-speed automatic
  • 0–60 mph: — 4.1 sec

Pros

  • B58 engine provides smooth, strong, and reliable power
  • Balanced mix of luxury and performance
  • All-weather confidence with xDrive AWD
  • More affordable than a full M3, with everyday usability

Consider

  • No manual transmission option
  • Not as extreme as the M3 for track use
  • Options and packages raise the price significantly
