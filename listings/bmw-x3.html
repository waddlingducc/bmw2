DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW X3 xDrive30i is a versatile compact luxury SUV that blends comfort, utility, and sporty handling. With standard all-wheel drive, a turbocharged 2.0L four-cylinder, and a spacious cabin, it’s an all-around choice for families and commuters who want BMW refinement in an SUV package.

Highlights

  • Turbocharged 2.0L four-cylinder with smooth power delivery
  • xDrive all-wheel drive standard for all-weather confidence
  • Balanced ride quality with sharp steering for its class
  • Spacious rear seat and ample cargo room
  • BMW curved display with latest iDrive infotainment

Specs

  • Powertrain: Turbo 2.0L inline-four (gas)
  • Drivetrain: xDrive AWD
  • Transmission: 8-speed automatic
  • 0–60 mph: — 6.0 sec

Pros

  • Strong balance of comfort and sporty handling
  • Standard AWD adds confidence in poor weather
  • Spacious interior and practical cargo space
  • Premium feel without being oversized

Consider

  • Not as powerful as higher trims (X3 M40i, X3 M)
  • Options and packages quickly raise the price
  • Firm ride compared to some rivals if equipped with sport suspension
