DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW 230i Coupe is a compact, rear-drive two-door that blends classic BMW proportions with modern tech. With a turbocharged 2.0-liter engine, balanced chassis, and sharp steering, it delivers engaging driving dynamics in an attainable package.

Highlights

  • Turbocharged 2.0L four-cylinder with smooth power delivery
  • Classic long-hood, short-deck BMW coupe proportions
  • Rear-wheel drive dynamics; xDrive AWD available in some trims
  • Available adaptive suspension and M Sport upgrades
  • Modern interior with curved display, iDrive, and driver assists

Specs

  • Powertrain: Turbo 2.0L inline-four (gas)
  • Drivetrain: RWD (AWD optional)
  • Transmission: 8-speed automatic
  • 0–60 mph: — 5.5 sec

Pros

  • Engaging handling and balanced chassis
  • Rear-drive fun in a compact, affordable BMW package
  • Up-to-date infotainment and connectivity features
  • Strong value compared with larger BMW models

Consider

  • Two-door body style limits rear-seat accessibility
  • Trunk space smaller than a sedan
  • For more power, shoppers may prefer the M240i
