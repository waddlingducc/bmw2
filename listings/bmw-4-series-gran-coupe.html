DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW 430i Gran Coupe blends the style of a coupe with the practicality of a hatchback. With four doors, a usable rear seat, and a wide-opening hatch, it offers more versatility than the 2-door coupe while keeping the same engaging handling and turbocharged performance.

Highlights

  • Turbocharged 2.0L inline-four with smooth, efficient power
  • Distinctive fastback profile with four doors
  • Hatchback design offers more cargo versatility
  • Modern cabin with curved display, iDrive 8, and premium finishes
  • Available with xDrive AWD for all-weather confidence

Specs

  • Powertrain: Turbo 2.0L inline-four (gas)
  • Drivetrain: RWD standard, AWD (xDrive) optional
  • Transmission: 8-speed automatic
  • 0–60 mph: — 5.8 sec

Pros

  • Sleek coupe-inspired design with four-door practicality
  • Extra cargo space and usability thanks to hatchback layout
  • Strong efficiency and smooth daily driving manners
  • High-tech interior with BMW’s latest infotainment

Consider

  • Less headroom in the rear due to sloping roofline
  • Not as powerful as M Performance or M variants
  • Higher price than a traditional sedan with similar space
