DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: Open-top two-seater blending classic roadster proportions with BMW M Performance punch. The Z4 M40i pairs the beloved B58 inline-six with a planted chassis and modern tech for top-down fun.

Highlights

  • B58 3.0L inline-six (M Performance tune)
  • Adaptive M suspension and M Sport differential
  • Driver-focused cockpit with iDrive and digital cluster
  • Power soft top, quick open/close

Specs

  • Powertrain: Turbo I6 (gas)
  • Drivetrain: RWD
  • 0–60 mph: — 4.2 sec

Pros

  • Classic roadster feel with modern comfort
  • Strong, smooth torque from the B58
  • Convertible top that doesn’t eat all the trunk

Consider

  • Two-seat layout only
  • Summer tires limit all-season flexibility
