DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW M850i xDrive Coupe is a grand tourer that combines elegant luxury with V8 performance. With a twin-turbocharged 4.4L V8, standard all-wheel drive, and a refined yet sporty chassis, it offers effortless high-speed cruising along with BMW’s hallmark driving excitement.

Highlights

  • 4.4L twin-turbo V8 with 523 hp
  • Standard xDrive AWD for confident traction
  • Elegant two-door coupe design with grand touring proportions
  • Adaptive M suspension and performance-tuned chassis
  • Luxurious interior with premium materials and iDrive infotainment

Specs

  • Powertrain: 4.4L twin-turbocharged V8 (gas)
  • Drivetrain: xDrive AWD
  • Transmission: 8-speed automatic
  • 0–60 mph: — 3.6 sec

Pros

  • Powerful V8 performance with smooth delivery
  • Luxury-focused cabin with advanced tech
  • Grand touring comfort for long-distance drives
  • Striking exterior design with strong road presence

Consider

  • Large coupe size makes it less agile than smaller M cars
  • Rear seats are tight and best for short trips
  • Premium price tag compared to less powerful 8 Series models
