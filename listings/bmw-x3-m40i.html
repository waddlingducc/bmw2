DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW X3 M40i is the M Performance version of BMW’s popular compact SUV. With the B58 turbocharged inline-six, adaptive suspension, and xDrive AWD, it delivers sharp handling and quick acceleration while keeping the practicality of a family-friendly SUV.

Highlights

  • B58 3.0L turbocharged inline-six with 393 hp
  • xDrive AWD with rear-bias for sporty dynamics
  • Adaptive M suspension and performance tuning
  • Spacious cabin with ample rear seat and cargo room
  • BMW curved display with iDrive infotainment

Specs

  • Powertrain: Turbocharged 3.0L I6 (gas)
  • Drivetrain: xDrive AWD
  • Transmission: 8-speed automatic
  • 0–60 mph: — 4.4 sec

Pros

  • Strong acceleration and engaging handling for an SUV
  • Everyday comfort blended with M Performance excitement
  • Spacious and practical interior for family use
  • All-weather capability thanks to AWD

Consider

  • Not as extreme as a full BMW X3 M
  • Firm ride compared to non-M X3 trims
  • Price rises quickly with performance and tech options
