DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW X1 M35i xDrive is the smallest M Performance SUV in BMW’s lineup, packing a punch with its turbocharged four-cylinder and sporty tuning. Compact yet practical, it delivers quick acceleration, precise handling, and everyday versatility in a city-friendly package.

Highlights

  • Turbocharged 2.0L inline-four with 313 hp
  • Standard xDrive all-wheel drive for confident traction
  • M Performance chassis tuning and sport exhaust
  • Compact SUV size with usable rear seat and cargo space
  • BMW curved display with iDrive infotainment and M-specific graphics

Specs

  • Powertrain: Turbocharged 2.0L I4 (gas)
  • Drivetrain: xDrive AWD
  • Transmission: 7-speed dual-clutch automatic
  • 0–60 mph: — 5.2 sec

Pros

  • Quick and agile for a compact luxury SUV
  • Standard AWD makes it capable in all seasons
  • Sporty styling and M-specific touches inside and out
  • More practical than a coupe or sedan while still fun to drive

Consider

  • Less powerful and refined than larger M Performance models
  • Compact size means tighter rear seat than midsize SUVs
  • No six-cylinder option available
