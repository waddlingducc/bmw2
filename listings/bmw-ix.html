DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW iX xDrive50 is BMW’s futuristic, all-electric midsize SUV that combines long range, high-tech luxury, and dual-motor AWD performance. With its spacious interior, sustainable materials, and advanced driver assistance, it’s a flagship EV SUV designed for comfort and capability.

Highlights

  • Dual-motor all-wheel drive with ~516 hp
  • Estimated 300 miles of EPA range
  • Fast charging up to 195 kW DC
  • Spacious, airy interior with sustainable materials
  • BMW curved display with iDrive 8.5 and advanced tech

Specs

  • Powertrain: Dual-motor electric AWD
  • Output: ~516 hp / ~564 lb-ft torque
  • Battery: ~111.5 kWh gross (~105 kWh usable)
  • Range: ~300 miles
  • 0–60 mph: — 4.4 sec

Pros

  • Spacious and ultra-modern cabin with luxury feel
  • Strong electric performance with AWD traction
  • High-tech infotainment and driver assistance suite
  • Good range and fast charging capability

Consider

  • Styling may be polarizing compared to traditional BMW SUVs
  • Heavier and less nimble than smaller BMW EVs
  • Premium price tag vs. competitors
