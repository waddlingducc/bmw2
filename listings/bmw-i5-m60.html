DESCRIPTION

Quick overview: The BMW i5 M60 is the performance-focused version of the all-electric 5 Series. With dual motors delivering M-tuned all-wheel drive, over 590 horsepower, and advanced chassis tech, it’s a luxury EV sedan that blends cutting-edge tech with true M Performance character.

Highlights

  • Dual-motor electric AWD with ~590 hp
  • M-tuned suspension, brakes, and steering
  • Estimated 250–280 miles of range (EPA est., verify on BMWUSA)
  • BMW curved display with iDrive 8.5 and M-specific graphics
  • Fast charging capability up to 205 kW DC

Specs

  • Powertrain: Dual-motor electric AWD
  • Output: ~590 hp / ~586 lb-ft torque
  • Battery: ~81 kWh usable capacity
  • Range: 270-295 miles
  • 0–60 mph: — 3.7 sec

Pros

  • Flagship performance in the all-electric 5 Series lineup
  • Instant torque and AWD grip
  • High-tech interior with luxury fit and finish
  • Fast charging enables long-distance usability

Consider

  • Less range than some non-performance EVs
  • Heavier than a gas-powered 5 Series
  • Premium pricing compared to the i5 eDrive40
